Undergraduate research

Optimal Client Selection in Federated Learning
2026 — Present

My B.Sc. research investigates client selection under non-IID data and communication budgets using information-theoretic generalization bounds and KL-divergence-based distribution mismatch. I am studying source-aware target-risk bounds, cost-aware sample allocation, and admission thresholds for excluding highly mismatched clients, while reproducing supporting numerical experiments.

Current graduate coursework
